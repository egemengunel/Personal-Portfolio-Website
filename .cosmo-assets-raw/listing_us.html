Transform Your Screen with Real Space Photography Cosmo Papers brings real astrophotography to your fingertips — galaxies, nebulae, black holes, and more, captured by professional and amateur astronomers around the world. Instantly preview any image as your lock screen or home screen wallpaper, then set it with one tap. Whether you want a glowing nebula behind your lock screen clock, a sweeping aurora for your home screen, or a slow scroll through the Milky Way, Cosmo Papers turns real space photography into wallpaper you'll actually want to look at. WHAT YOU CAN DO Browse real astrophotography across 19 curated categories — galaxies, nebulae, aurora, black holes, planets, and more Preview any wallpaper on your actual lock screen, including the Liquid Glass clock Save your favorite wallpapers to a personal collection Add a Home Screen widget that rotates real space photography Available on iPhone and iPad WHY COSMO PAPERS? Most wallpaper apps recycle generic stock photos or AI-generated art. Cosmo Papers is built entirely on real astrophotography, organized into a taxonomy you can actually browse by subject — not an endless, undifferentiated scroll. If you want your phone to look like deep space, not a stock photo folder, this is the difference. PERFECT FOR Space and astronomy enthusiasts who want the real thing, not AI renders Anyone who wants a clean, categorized wallpaper library instead of infinite scrolling iPhone and iPad users who want a Liquid Glass lock screen that actually matches their wallpaper Minimalists who want quality over an endless scroll of mediocre wallpapers SUBSCRIPTION DETAILS Cosmo Plus unlocks the full experience: • The full archive, back to 1995 — not just the recent collection • Every wallpaper category, including Aurora, Black Holes, Hubble & Webb, and more • Original, full-resolution saves • Smart search — describe what you're picturing instead of guessing keywords • Translate descriptions into your language • A live Home Screen widget with real space photography, not a placeholder Available as an auto-renewable subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase: • Plus Weekly – $6.99 / week • Plus Yearly – $19.99 / year (3-day free trial) • Plus Lifetime – $39.99 one-time purchase Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase.
